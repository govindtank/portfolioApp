Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 17, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 22, 2026

1. Introduction & Application Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Govind Tank Portfolio mobile application (Package: com.govindtank.portfolio), developed by Govind Tank and distributed via the Google Play Store.

The application serves solely as a personal developer portfolio and interactive professional resume showcasing technical projects, experience, publications, and skills.

2. Information Collection & Use

We do not collect, process, store, or transmit any user personal information. Specifically:

  • No Personal Data: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, or physical addresses.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or persistent hardware identifiers.
  • No Precise Location: We do not request or access GPS, network-based location, or Wi-Fi scan data.
  • No Account Required: You can access and explore all features of the application without creating an account or logging in.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The application does not integrate third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or behavior profiling tools.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

The application requests minimal standard device capabilities:

  • Internet Access: Used solely to allow users to optionally open external public portfolio links (such as GitHub repositories, LinkedIn profiles, or live demo sites) in their device's default web browser.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The application does not request camera, microphone, storage, contacts, SMS, or biometric sensor access.

4. Third-Party Links & Services

The application contains outbound links to external third-party platforms (including GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and the Google Play Store). If you choose to follow these external links, you will leave the application and navigate to those third-party services. Those platforms maintain their own independent privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because the application does not collect, record, or store any personal data on remote servers or local databases, there is no user data retained, and no data deletion requests are necessary.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The application does not address or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). As no personal data is collected from any user, the application is safe for all audiences.

7. Security

Although we collect no personal data, the application is built following industry-standard Android security best practices and modern development standards.

We may periodically review and update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date.
